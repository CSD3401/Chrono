=============================================
Game Concept
=============================================
First-person puzzle game set in a facility post-explosion. Use the ability to switch between the past and present timelines to solve puzzles and uncover clues.

=============================================
Controls
=============================================
W - Move forward
A - Move left
S - Move backward
D - Move right
Mouse - Look around
SPACEBAR - Jump
E - Interact (open doors, etc.)
Q - Activate/Deactivate Chrono (switch between past and present)
Mouse Scroll - Rotate Bomb puzzle elements (when in interaction mode)
Left Click - Enter/Exit interaction mode on puzzles, Click on interable notes
